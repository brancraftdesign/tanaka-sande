<p class="desc">Eden is a desk monitor arm designed for a client in China who wanted it to feel automotive. The arm is built from continuous surface transitions, the way a car body is, so each section flows into the next instead of being bolted to it. The elbow joints are hollow: the bearings and torsion system sit inside them, and a copper sleeve lines each opening, the one warm detail on a quiet object. A fine ring marks where the column turns. I took it from sketches through Fusion 360 CAD to KeyShot renders and the client presentation, and explored it in five finishes, from silver to black.</p>
