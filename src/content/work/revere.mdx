<Sec n="02" title="The idea">
  <p>Most fashion brands begin with a logo and a trend. REVERE began with a conviction: how a man dresses is a form of respect, for himself, for the people around him and for the occasion.</p>
  <p class="quote">People notice care long before they can name it.</p>
  <p>The name carries two meanings: to revere, and the revere collar, the open collar of the era the brand draws from. The line drawings carry the jazz; the tailoring carries the old money. Inside each piece sits one small detail only the wearer knows.</p>
</Sec>

<Sec n="03" title="A record label">
  <p>Jazz is the method, not the subject. A jazz musician composes from whatever is in the air, so REVERE's graphics answer the mood of a moment. The brand releases clothing the way a label releases records: drops are albums, pieces are tracks, and every piece has a catalogue number.</p>
  <figure><ReleaseCard /><figcaption><span>Release card and mood tracklist · Drop 01</span></figcaption></figure>
</Sec>

<Sec n="04" title="Identity">
  <p>A lowercase wordmark that stays quiet so the graphics can speak. Onyx and ivory do most of the work; tobacco, slate and sand come from the clothes themselves. Instrument Serif sets the titles, and DM Mono sets catalogue numbers, the way a record sleeve sets its credits.</p>
  <div class="grid-2"><WordmarkPlate /><TypePlate /></div>
</Sec>
