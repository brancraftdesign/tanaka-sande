<p class="desc">REVERE is a menswear label built on one idea: how a man dresses is a form of respect, for himself, for the people around him and for the occasion. The name holds two meanings, to revere and the revere collar, and the brand sits between old-money tailoring and jazz-age ease. Jazz is the method rather than the subject, so REVERE releases clothing the way a label releases records: drops are albums, pieces are tracks, and every piece carries a catalogue number. I designed the identity, the line-and-colour figure library, the release system and its motion. The identity is complete, and the first pieces are in development for a small private circle.</p>
