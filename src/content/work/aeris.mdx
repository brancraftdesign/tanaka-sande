<p class="desc">Aeris is a smart humidifier designed to live in a room like an object, not an appliance. The body is a soft, squared cylinder in a speckled finish, with a translucent band around the tank so the water level shows at a glance. It fills from the top: lift the half-moon lid and pour water straight in. A small touch panel reads and sets the humidity, power comes in through USB-C, and the unit connects over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. I designed the product in three colourways, slate, off-white and coral, along with the AERIS identity, the PureMist HM-S01 packaging and the brand's touchpoints.</p>
