<p class="desc">Aeris is a smart humidifier for the dry Southern African savannah, where indoor humidity often falls below the comfortable 40–60%. I surveyed 47 people and studied humidifiers from seven brands, including Dyson, Levoit and Venta, and the same complaints kept coming back: noise, limescale and awkward cleaning. I chose a focused first product, a quiet standalone humidifier rather than a costlier humidifier-purifier, and took three CAD concepts forward: the first, with a separate docking station, cost too much to make; the second felt too sharp; the third, soft, compact and easy to carry, met the brief. It fills from the top through a half-moon lid, and holds an ultrasonic nebulizer, a replaceable demineralizer and USB-C power inside a speckled, recyclable shell in dark air force blue, off-white and pale orange. I also named the brand, from the Latin for air, and designed its identity, the PureMist HM-S01 packaging and its touchpoints.</p>
