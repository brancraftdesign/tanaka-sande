<p class="desc">BLOO is a coffee brand I'm building for a distracted world: a calm, Afro-urban café concept made for focus, where people come to work, think clearly and do their best work. Most coffee shops sell atmosphere; BLOO sells concentration, so the identity stays quiet on purpose. A custom-drawn, tall and condensed wordmark sits on Yale blue and porcelain, with two lighter blues for depth, Lilex for detail and Inter for reading. I also drew the brand's people: on calls, writing, presenting, always with a cup within reach, in a single blue so the system stays calm wherever it appears.</p>
