<p class="desc">BLOO is a coffee brand I'm building for a distracted world: a calm, Afro-urban café concept made for focus, where people come to work, think clearly and do their best work. Most coffee shops sell atmosphere; BLOO sells concentration, so the identity stays quiet on purpose. A custom-drawn, tall and condensed wordmark sits on Yale blue and porcelain, with two lighter blues for depth, Lilex for detail and Inter for reading. On the cup, the wordmark is cropped by the cup's own edges and sits above a quiet line of words: Balance, Lucid, Continuum, Rhythm. I modelled and rendered the cups and a wrap-around two-cup carrier in 3D, carrying the line Focus, by design.</p>
